Day 68–72

I've officially passed the halfway mark of this 143-day barefoot walking practice. Day 72 of 143.

This past week was exceptionally busy at Wutai Shan, with packed public Sound Meditation sessions, full small-group sessions, a large group of short-term monastics staying for the week, and a constant stream of visitors coming through the gates. With so much happening, I simply didn't spend as much time walking as I had hoped, and I've fallen behind the pace I need to maintain for my goal.

Now that I'm halfway through, it's time to make up some ground. This week I'll be pushing myself toward longer distances and challenging myself to set some new personal bests, although I still have a few busy days ahead at Wutai Shan.
