Livestream on [youtube.com/@bhantebodhicitta](https://www.youtube.com/@bhantebodhicitta)

This Saturday, I will be walking 108 kilometres from Cham Shan Temple to Wutai Shan, and I have decided to make this journey an opportunity to share the Dhamma in a different way.

Throughout the walk, I will be livestreaming 108 individual contemplations inspired by the Four Noble Truths, the foundation of the Buddha’s teachings.

We will explore the nature of suffering, the causes of suffering, the possibility of its cessation, and the path leading to liberation. Each kilometre will offer an opportunity to reflect on a different aspect of the mind, from craving, aversion, and attachment to mindfulness, compassion, wisdom, and letting go.

My intention is to make these teachings accessible to everyone, including those who have never studied Buddhism. You do not need to be Buddhist to participate. These are reflections on the human experience, the challenges we share, and the possibility of cultivating greater understanding and peace in our lives.

I will be sharing all 108 livestreams on YouTube, so you can join me along the way, wherever you are in the world.

This walk is part of my broader vision for All Seasons Peace Walks, an initiative inspired by the belief that peace is something we cultivate through our conduct, our intentions, and the way we live with one another.

The destination is Wutai Shan. The deeper intention is to walk the path taught by the Buddha, one step and one contemplation at a time.

I invite you to walk with me.

May we cultivate peace within ourselves and bring greater peace into the world.

108 kilometres. 108 opportunities to contemplate the Dhamma. One journey toward understanding and liberation.
