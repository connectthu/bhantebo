A Path of Practice

Study, practice and sharing the Dhamma

The Buddha's teachings offer a practical path towards understanding the nature of life, cultivating the mind and bringing an end to suffering. As a Theravāda Buddhist novice monk, Venerable Bodhicitta dedicates his life to the study and practice of the Dhamma. His work explores traditional Buddhist meditation, attentive listening, mindful walking and the many ways these practices can be shared with others.

This website is a place to explore that journey, discover opportunities for practice and learn about the activities through which the Dhamma is shared.

A journey of 108 kilometres

One step at a time

The barefoot Peace Walk from Cham Shan in Markham to Wutai Shan brought pilgrimage into everyday movement. The journey asked for patience and perseverance, and was shaped by the landscapes and people met along the way.
