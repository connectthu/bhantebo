<section id="journey-108" class="section wrap">
  <div class="eyebrow">One Step at a Time</div><h2>The 108 km Peace Walk</h2>
  <p class="lede">The completed barefoot journey from Cham Shan in Markham to Wutai Shan was a practice in patience and perseverance. The walk passed through urban and rural landscapes, with support from police officers and community members. Along the way were moments of rest at the monastery, sunrise and moonset, supporters joining the route, and arrival at Wutai Shan for a welcoming ceremony at the Main Buddha Hall.</p>

  <p class="lede">All Season Peace Walks brings people together through mindful walking, pilgrimage and a shared aspiration for peace. The inaugural 108 km walk in 2027 is in development. Dates, route, destination and participation details will be shared when they are confirmed.</p>
