<section id="buddhist-meditation" class="section wrap">
  <div class="eyebrow">Buddhist Meditation</div><h2>Cultivating the mind</h2>
  <p class="lede">Meditation is an integral part of the Buddha's path. Traditional practices support the cultivation of concentration, mindfulness, insight and wisdom, within the wider training of ethical conduct and understanding.</p>
  <div class="content-grid content-grid--three">
    <article class="content-card"><h3>Samatha</h3><p>Tranquillity meditation develops steadiness and collectedness of mind.</p></article>
    <article class="content-card"><h3>Vipassanā</h3><p>Insight meditation supports direct investigation of experience and the nature of life.</p></article>
    <article class="content-card"><h3>Ānāpānasati</h3><p>Mindfulness of breathing uses the breath as a foundation for sustained awareness.</p></article>
    <article class="content-card"><h3>Satipaṭṭhāna</h3><p>The foundations of mindfulness bring careful attention to body, feeling, mind and experience.</p></article>
    <article class="content-card"><h3>Mettā</h3><p>Loving-kindness practice cultivates goodwill and care for oneself and others.</p></article>
    <article class="content-card"><h3>Brahmavihāra</h3><p>The divine abidings are qualities of loving-kindness, compassion, appreciative joy and equanimity.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

<section id="sound-meditation" class="section wrap">
  <div class="eyebrow">Listening · Stillness · Awareness</div><h2>Guanyin Sound Meditation</h2>
  <p class="lede">Guanyin Sound Meditation is a contemplative practice using sound as an object of attentive awareness. Sound instruments create conditions conducive to stillness, meditation and contemplation. The practice gives attention to listening, silence and awareness.</p>
  <div class="about-grid">
    <img src="{{ '/assets/images/reflection-photo.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Sound meditation instruments arranged for a contemplative practice" loading="lazy">
    <div><h3>Instruments and attentive listening</h3><p class="body-text">The practice may include Tibetan singing bowls, crystal singing bowls, gongs, an Ocean Drum, Koshi chimes and tuning forks. Their sounds are approached as a focus for awareness, with silence as an important part of the practice.</p><p class="body-text">Venerable Bodhicitta has shared sound-based contemplative meditation practices in educational and community settings, bringing opportunities for attentive listening, stillness and reflection to people from diverse backgrounds.</p><ul><li>Emmanuel College, Victoria University, University of Toronto</li><li>Western University, London, Ontario</li><li>Trinity College, Port Hope, Ontario</li><li>Senior and long-term care communities</li></ul></div>
  </div>
</section>
